Help

Updates, undo and fixes

What happens after setup, how to go back to the TV's own home screen, and what to do when something isn't right.

Updates

  • Twice a day the Fan Hub looks for a newer release on GitHub. It downloads it, checks it's signed with den-tv's own key, and installs it while nobody is watching Home or the Fan Hub.
  • A release waits 3 days before TVs install it, so a bad one can be pulled first. Check now on the phone (TV tab › Updates) gets it sooner; Get releases first makes a TV install each one straight away.
  • On Android 12 and later it's silent. The very first update may ask once: the Fan Hub's footer says Update ready · OK installs it now. On older TVs Android asks every time.
  • Your looks, settings and keys stay through every update.

Built to fail safely

Checks before it changes Home

Setup starts den-tv first, and only switches Google's home screen off once den-tv is running. If Home doesn't stick, it puts Google's back.

Keeps your Wi-Fi

On TVs where Google's setup app saved the Wi-Fi network, it stops rather than let Android forget the network.

Safe mode

Two crashes within ten minutes and den-tv starts as a plain home screen, with your apps and no looks, until a fix arrives or you pick a look.

Nothing removed

Google's home screen and any sped-up apps are switched off, not uninstalled, so each comes back with one switch.

Only tested speed-ups

No list for your TV, nothing switched off. Core Android, the HDMI inputs' software, Settings and the keyboard are never touched.

USB debugging off

Setup turns it off again at the end and throws away its key.

Undo

  1. Turn USB debugging on again (setup step 2).
  2. Open the Fan Hub and hold OK for a second. (Or on the phone: TV tab › Home screen › Open it on the TV.)
  3. Choose Give Home back to Google and allow the prompt. It switches Google's home screen and any sped-up apps back on, then turns USB debugging off.
  4. To remove the apps too: SettingsAppsden-tvUninstall, and the same for the Fan Hub.

From a computer, the installer undoes it too: see Undo in INSTALL.md.

If Home stops working

  1. Open Settings: the remote's gear button, the gear on den-tv's top bar, or say "open settings" to the Assistant.
  2. Go to AppsSee all appsShow system apps.
  3. Open Google TV Home (or Android TV Home) and choose Enable. Press ⌂ Home.

As a last resort, a factory reset (SettingsSystemReset) brings the TV back as it came.

Common problems

The Fan Hub says nothing answers on port 5555

This TV only listens for USB debugging over a cable. Install from a computer or an Android phone instead: ways B to D in INSTALL.md.

"Allow USB debugging?" never appears

Check USB debugging is still on and choose Try again. Switching it off and on again, or restarting the TV, usually brings the question back.

"The Wi-Fi network this TV is on was saved by Google's setup"

Android would delete that network if Google's setup were switched off, so den-tv stopped before changing anything. In SettingsNetwork & Internet, choose your network, Forget it, join it again with its password, and run the setup again. Or connect a network cable.

The TV only has "Wireless debugging"

Newer TVs pair with a code instead. Use a computer: the steps are in INSTALL.md.

Home goes back to Google's screen

Run the setup again (hold OK on the Fan Hub, Set it up again). Some makers lock the home screen; setup then puts the TV's own home back, so nothing is broken. Please open an issue on GitHub with your TV model.

"Signed with a different key"

An older test build of den-tv is on the TV. Undo, uninstall it, then install again.

The phone page doesn't open

The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, not mobile data or a guest network. Open the Fan Hub to see the current address and code.

Back, Home or typing from the phone does nothing

Those need the den-tv helper: on the TV, SettingsAccessibilityden-tv helperOn.

Scores only appear after the match

Live scores need the free football-data.org and CricketData.org keys. Check their rows on the phone show Working.

The TV feels slow

Try Tweak › Home › Motion: Calm, and TV tab › Memory › Close background apps. If your TV model has a tested list, hold OK on the Fan Hub and choose Speed up this TV.

Privacy

  • Your settings, looks, photos, characters and keys stay on the TV. There's no account and no den-tv server.
  • The Fan Hub asks the public services on the credits page for scores, episodes, weather, prices and headlines, and GitHub for updates. Gemini gets what you type in Describe a look, if you add a key.
  • The phone page only works on your home network, after pairing with the code on the TV.
  • The helper only notes which app is in front, for screen time and the kids timer. It reads nothing else on screen.

Questions

Does it work on Samsung, LG or Fire TV?

No. It needs Android TV or Google TV: Sony, TCL, Hisense, Xiaomi, Vu, OnePlus, Philips, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield and most others.

Will I lose my apps or logins?

No. Your apps, logins, Live TV, HDMI inputs, Google Assistant and casting stay as they were.

Does it cost anything?

No. den-tv is free and open source, and the optional keys are free too.

Can I add my own player or character?

Yes: describe a look with Gemini, save a photo as a look, or upload a character on the phone. They stay on your TV. The looks den-tv ships are drawn in code, with no photos or official artwork.

How do I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub with your TV model and what happened.
