For Android TV and Google TV

Your TV, dressed for your teams and your heroes

den-tv replaces the TV's ad-filled home screen with one that's yours: Itachi's Sharingan following the app you pick, Sachin's straight drive racing to it, Camp Nou on match night. Live scores and next episodes come to Home, and your phone runs it all.

What it does

60+ looks

Football, cricket, One Piece, Dragon Ball, Naruto, Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen and more. Each one is a wallpaper, a watcher, a clock, icon styles, a layout and a wake animation, all drawn in code.

Looks ›

Describe a look

Type "Raphinha in Barça colours, a night at the Camp Nou" and, with a free Gemini key, den-tv designs the look and paints its wallpaper.

How it works ›

Scores and episodes

Your teams' next matches with a countdown, the live score during play, and new episodes of your shows the day they air.

Fan Hub ›

Big moments

When your team scores, Home plays GOAL! with confetti. Fifty runs sends the ball out of the ground. A new episode slides its card in.

Moments ›

12 kinds of widget

Sensex and Nifty, crypto, a team's fixtures, Formula 1, weather, air quality, holidays, your calendar, headlines and a birthday countdown.

Widgets ›

A phone remote

Pick looks, type into search boxes, switch HDMI inputs, set a sleep timer and a kids timer, and see screen time, from any phone on your Wi-Fi.

Phone app ›

Everyone gets their own

Each person has a name, a colour and their own look. A guest sees only looks for everyone, and a private profile is PIN-locked.

People ›

A faster TV

Google's home screen is switched off, and on TV models that cleanyourtv has tested, the background apps that slow it down go too.

Speeding it up ›

Keeps itself up to date

New versions install by themselves when nobody's watching, three days after release. Two crashes and it starts in safe mode.

Updates and safety ›

When it matters, Home celebrates

Live moments need the free score keys (how to get them). Without them, scores arrive when the match ends.

How you get it

1. Two downloads on the TV

Turn on USB debugging, install the free Downloader app, and type the code 3476905. It opens den-tv's downloads.

2. A few presses of OK

The Fan Hub makes den-tv the home screen by itself, asks about the extras, and turns USB debugging off again.

3. Pair your phone

Scan the QR code on the TV, pick a look, add your teams and shows. Done.

Built on cleanyourtv

The safe, careful part is cleanyourtv's

den-tv began as a fork of cleanyourtv by Gathin, which cleans up an Android TV safely and replaces its home screen. The launcher underneath den-tv, the safety checks in setup, and the tested speed-up lists for over 350 TV models are cleanyourtv's work and its contributors'. den-tv adds the looks, the Fan Hub and the phone app on top.
