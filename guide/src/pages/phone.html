Phone app

Your TV, from your phone

The TV serves a web page to any phone on your Wi-Fi. There's nothing to install from an app store: pair once, add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app whenever the TV is on.

Pair a phone

  1. Open the Fan Hub on the TV.
  2. Scan its QR code with the phone's camera, or type the address shown under it into the browser.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code from the TV (it changes every 5 minutes) and give the phone a name.
  4. Add the page to your home screen: Share › Add to Home Screen on iPhone, ⋮ › Add to Home screen on Android.

Only phones on the same Wi-Fi can reach the page, and only after pairing. The TV's Menu button on the Fan Hub forgets every paired phone; the phone's TV tab › Paired phones removes one.

Six tabs

Looks

  • Every look, by category or search, with a star for favourites. Surprise me puts on a random one.
  • Describe a look in words; with a Gemini key, Gemini designs it and paints its wallpaper.
  • Make your own look from any look's wallpaper and your own parts, or Save a photo as a look.
  • Your photos as the wallpaper, and Your characters: a picture that becomes a figure watching the row.
  • Add a shared look from a friend's code or file, and share yours.

Tweak

  • This look: its watcher, clock, app icons, layout, focus effect, wake animation, extras and colour. Restore to default look or Save as my own look.
  • Home: your name in the greeting, the match panel, motion (Full, Calm or Off), animations for opening apps, text contrast, the idle clock and screen care.
  • Widgets on Home and Your widgets.
  • Apps on Home: hide, show and rename apps, your own picture for any app, a Shows folder, badges, and sorting the row by what you watch.
  • Fan Hub: the teams and shows you follow.

Auto

Looks that change by themselves and come back to yours after: your team's match day, India and IPL games, a new episode, late night, Diwali and New Year's Eve, weekend mornings, and Surprise me each morning.

People

  • Who's watching: each person has a name for the greeting, a colour and their own look. Switch from the phone or the TV's Looks screen.
  • Guest: only looks for everyone, no scores and no name, until the TV sleeps.
  • Private profile: PIN-locked, with photos that show only while that person is watching. Five wrong tries lock it for 10 minutes.
  • Hide an app for one person only, and see screen time per person.

Remote

  • What's on the TV now, for how long, and who's watching.
  • Volume, mute, play and pause, Back, Home and sleep, plus a sleep timer.
  • Type on the TV: select a search box on the TV, type on the phone, send.
  • Switch HDMI inputs, open any app, and Watch now for a live match or a new episode.

TV

  • Wi-Fi and network: signal strength and a speed test.
  • Keys and live data: add the free keys, see whether each works, and try a moment on Home.
  • Memory and storage: close background apps (except ones you keep running) and see what's using space.
  • Screen time per app and per person, a kids timer, and quiet hours.
  • Updates: versions, Check now, Install now, and getting releases first.
  • Home screen and Speed up: which apps were switched off, and buttons that open the setup on the TV.
  • Paired phones.

The den-tv helper

Back, Home, sleep, typing, opening apps, screen time and the kids timer need the helper, an accessibility service that only notes which app is in front. You turn it on during setup, or later on the TV: SettingsAccessibilityden-tv helperOn. Volume and play/pause work without it.

What no app can do on Android TV: wake the TV from the phone, or press the arrow keys inside other apps. The Google TV app on your phone does both.
