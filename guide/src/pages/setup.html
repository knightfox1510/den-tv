Setup guide

Put den-tv on your TV

A home screen for football, cricket and anime fans, with live scores and next episodes, looks you can design by describing them, and a phone remote. This guide does it with just the TV remote, in nine steps.

About 10 minutes
Free
Undo any time
Keep your phone next to you to follow along

Will it work on my TV?

Works

Any Android TV or Google TV: Sony, TCL, Hisense, Xiaomi, Vu, OnePlus, Philips, Toshiba, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield and most others.

If the TV's Settings has a page called About with Android TV OS on it, you're good.

Doesn't work

Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Amazon Fire TV and Apple TV run other systems.

The TV needs Wi-Fi or a network cable, and about 150 MB free.

1

Turn on Developer options

A hidden menu in Settings. It's needed so the Fan Hub can switch the home screen for you.

  1. Press ⌂ Home, then open Settings (the gear, top right).
  2. Go to SystemAbout.
    Older TVs: Device PreferencesAbout
  3. Scroll down to Android TV OS build (or just Build) and press OK on it 7 times.
  4. The TV says "You are now a developer!" If it asks for your PIN, enter it.
1234567
2

Turn on USB debugging

The Fan Hub uses it for about a minute, then turns it off again by itself.

  1. Press Back once to System. A new item is there: Developer options.
    Older TVs: Device PreferencesDeveloper options
  2. Open it and switch on USB debugging. Choose OK if it asks.
  3. If you also see Network debugging or ADB over network, switch that on too.

USB debugging lets a trusted device control the TV. The Fan Hub switches it off when it's finished, and you'll see a tick for it.

3

Install Downloader

A free app for downloading files on a TV. You only need it today.

  1. Press ⌂ Home and open the Play Store (Google TV: the Apps tab, then search).
  2. Search for Downloader. Pick the one by AFTVnews (an orange icon with a down arrow).
  3. Choose Install, then Open. Allow it to use storage if it asks.
4

Type the code 3476905

It opens den-tv's download page on GitHub.

  1. In Downloader, select the address box on the Home tab.
  2. Type 3476905 with the number keys on screen and choose Go.
  3. A page called Releases opens. The newest release is at the top.

No luck with the code? Type github.com/knightfox1510/den-tv/releases instead.

5

Install den-tv

The home screen itself. Install it first, and don't open it yet.

  1. On the Releases page, under the newest release, choose den-tv-launcher.apk. It downloads.
  2. Choose Install.
  3. The first time, the TV says it isn't allowed to install apps from Downloader. Choose Settings, switch Downloader on, press Back, and choose Install again.
  4. When it says App installed, choose Done, not Open.

If Play Protect asks about an unknown app, choose Install anyway. den-tv isn't on the Play Store, so Google doesn't know it yet. Its code is public on GitHub.

6

Install the Fan Hub, and open it

Scores, episodes, widgets, updates and the phone app. It sets everything else up.

  1. Press Back to the Releases page and choose den-tv-fanhub.apk.
  2. Choose Install, and this time choose Open.
7

Make den-tv your home screen

The Fan Hub asks a few questions, then does the rest by itself. Use ◀ ▶ and OK.

Press ⌂ Home on the remote: den-tv opens. If the TV asks which app to use for Home, choose den-tv and Always.

8

Pair your phone

The phone is where you pick looks, add your teams and widgets, and use it as a remote. Same Wi-Fi as the TV.

  1. Open the Fan Hub from its tile on Home.
  2. On your phone, scan the QR code on the TV with the camera, or type the address shown under it (like 192.168.1.23:8765) into the browser.
  3. Type the code from the TV. It changes now and then, so use the one on screen.
  4. Add the page to your home screen: Share › Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or ⋮ › Add to Home screen on Android.
9

Make it yours

All on the phone page. Everything changes on the TV within a minute. Everything the phone page does.

  • Looks tab: pick a look (a wallpaper, clock, watcher and moments), or Describe a look in your own words. With a free Gemini key it designs a new one, wallpaper included.
  • Fan Hub: add your teams and shows. Scores, countdowns and big moments (GOAL!, SIX!) follow them.
  • Tweak › Your widgets: markets, crypto, a team's fixtures, Formula 1, weather, air quality, holidays or your calendar, headlines, a countdown to a birthday.
  • TV tab › Keys and live data: optional free keys for Gemini and live scores. Each shows whether it's working. How to get them.

Speeding the TV up

Switching Google's home screen off is already the biggest saving. On top of that, the Fan Hub can switch off apps that run in the background (ads, tracking, store demos, auto-installs), but only on TV models where the cleanyourtv project has tested a list. The wrong app off can cost a TV its HDMI inputs, so it never guesses.

Apps are switched off, not removed. Apps with their own tile start unticked, and the phone's TV tab › Speed up shows each one with a button to switch it back on.

Already ran cleanyourtv before? The Fan Hub says the TV is already sped up.

After setup

Updates

The Fan Hub checks for new versions of both apps and installs them when the TV is quiet, waiting three days after each release in case it has a problem. To get one sooner, open the phone page's TV tab › Updates and choose Check now.

Undo: get the TV's own home screen back

Open the Fan Hub, hold OK for a second, and choose Give Home back to Google. (Or on the phone: TV tab › Home screen › Open it on the TV.) It needs USB debugging on again for a minute, and switches it off after. Then, to remove the apps too: SettingsAppsden-tvUninstall, and the same for the Fan Hub.

What changes on the TV, and what doesn't

Changes: den-tv becomes Home; Google's home screen is switched off; with your OK, the helper, the screensaver, and the tested speed-up apps. USB debugging is switched off at the end.

Doesn't change: your apps and logins, Live TV and HDMI inputs, Google Assistant, casting, and Settings. Nothing is uninstalled.

Privacy: your settings, keys and photos stay on the TV. The phone page only works on your home Wi-Fi, with the code from the TV.

If something goes wrong

The Fan Hub says nothing answers on port 5555

Your TV only listens for USB debugging over a cable. Use a computer or an Android phone instead (see Other ways). Both take about the same time.

"Allow USB debugging?" never appears

Check USB debugging is still on (step 2), then choose Try again. On some TVs, switching USB debugging off and on again makes the question appear.

It stopped because the setup wizard runs the Wi-Fi

On a few TVs the first-time setup app also looks after Wi-Fi, so switching it off would cut the TV off. Connect with a network cable, or choose Open Wi-Fi settings and forget and rejoin your network, then try again.

The Home button still opens the old home screen

Run step 7 again: open the Fan Hub, hold OK for a second, and choose Set it up again (or Make it my home screen). If the TV asks which app to use for Home, choose den-tv and Always.

den-tv keeps crashing, or Home is blank

After two crashes in ten minutes, den-tv starts in safe mode: a plain home screen with your apps, and no looks or animations. Picking a look tries again, and an update with a fix ends it.

To get Google's home screen back without a computer, open Settings (the remote's gear button, or say "open settings" to the Assistant), then AppsSee all appsShow system appsGoogle TV HomeEnable (or Android TV Home), and press ⌂ Home.

The phone page doesn't open

The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, not mobile data or a guest network. Open the Fan Hub to see the current address and code.
