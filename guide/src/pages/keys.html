Free keys

Three free keys, each optional

den-tv works without any key. A key is a password a service gives you so den-tv can ask it for more: Gemini designs looks, and the two score services send live scores during play. Each takes a few minutes and costs nothing.

Keep keys private

  • Paste a key only into den-tv's phone page. Never post it in a chat, an issue, a screenshot or a GitHub file.
  • Keys are stored on the TV only. The phone page shows just the last four characters, like ••••x9Q2, so you can tell which key it is.
  • If a key leaks, delete it on the service's website and make a new one, then use Replace on the phone.

Gemini

  1. On a phone or computer, open aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Accept the terms if it asks.
  3. Choose Create API key. If it asks for a project, let it create one.
  4. Copy the key. It's a long line starting with AIza.
  5. On den-tv's phone page: TV tab › Keys and live data › Gemini, paste it and choose Add the key. The TV tries it straight away and shows Working.

Under the key there's a Gemini model choice. Flash (newest) is the default, Flash-Lite is the fastest, and Pro is the slowest and smartest. If your key can't use a model, den-tv falls back to one it can.

Painting wallpapers uses Gemini's image model, which some free keys can't use. The Gemini row then says so, and den-tv draws a poster in the look's colours instead. What you type in Describe a look is sent to Google; on the free tier Google may use it to improve its products, so keep it to looks.

football-data.org

  1. Open football-data.org/client/register.
  2. Enter your name and email, accept the terms and register.
  3. Open the email they send. Your key is in it, labelled as your API token: a line of 32 letters and digits.
  4. On den-tv's phone page: TV tab › Keys and live data › football-data.org, paste it and choose Add the key.

The free plan covers the top European leagues, the Champions League and the World Cup. Matches in other leagues still show, with their scores after the final whistle.

CricketData.org

  1. Open cricketdata.org and choose Sign up (free).
  2. Confirm your email and sign in.
  3. Your API key is on your dashboard. Copy it.
  4. On den-tv's phone page: TV tab › Keys and live data › CricketData.org, paste it and choose Add the key.

100 calls a day is enough for a match day. The CricketData row shows how many are used today; when they run out, scores pick up again the next day.

Is my key working?

Each key has a row on the phone's TV tab › Keys and live data. The TV checks a key when you add it, every time it uses it, and once a day.

ShowsMeansWhat to do
WorkingThe service answered, with when it was last checked.Nothing.
Problem"Refused this key": wrong or deleted key.Copy it again from the service, or make a new one, then Replace.
Problem"Today's free calls are used up".Nothing. It works again tomorrow.
Problem"The TV couldn't reach…"Check the TV's internet, then Test.
Not addedNo key yet.Optional: follow the steps above.

Test checks a key now, Replace swaps it, and Remove deletes it from the TV.
