The foundation

cleanyourtv, by Gathin

cleanyourtv cleans up an Android TV safely and replaces its ad-filled home screen with a clean, fast one. den-tv started as a fork of it, and kept these parts as they are:

  • The launcher underneath den-tv's looks: the dock and grid, D-pad navigation, the Inputs and Wi-Fi pills, Launcher settings and the remote-button helpers. den-tv's own code sits in separate files with small hooks, so cleanyourtv's later fixes can come in.
  • The safety rules in setup: which core packages are never touched, the checks for the setup wizard, inputs and Settings, and switching the home screen over without leaving the TV without one.
  • The speed-up lists for over 350 TV models, each tested on that model by its owner. They're cleanyourtv's work and its contributors'.

If you only want a clean, fast TV without den-tv's looks, use cleanyourtv. If your TV isn't on its lists yet, its guide shows how to test and add it, and den-tv picks it up in a later release.

Fan tributes

Every scene, poster and watcher in den-tv is drawn in code. Players' and characters' names label fan tributes; den-tv isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any club, league, broadcaster, studio or publisher, and ships no photos or official artwork. Photos you add stay on your own TV.
