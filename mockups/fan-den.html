The Home screen

This is the cleanyourtv launcher with a theme of your own. It needs no new code: a config.json and three wallpapers that change with the time of day (trail dawn in the morning, anime dusk in the evening, match night after dark).

The cricket line under the clock is the repo's Game Day add-on. It reads your teams' calendar feeds and counts down to the next game. It never shows scores, so nothing gets spoiled.

New add-on to build

Fan Hub

The launcher deliberately never touches the network, so live scores can't go on Home itself. Fan Hub would be a small separate app that sits in the dock as a tile, built the same way as the repo's Weather and News add-ons.

  • Cricket and football: a scores API. Most need a free key, and some limit requests per day.
  • Anime: AniList's airing schedule. Free, no key needed.
  • Trails: Open-Meteo forecasts. Free, no key needed.
  • "Next 72 hours" merges all of these into one list, so you can see whether Sunday's hike clashes with kickoff.
  • It only fetches while it's on screen, so it uses no data in the background on a TV with 1 GB of RAM.

To make it yours

  1. Teams: done. India, Mumbai Indians, FC Barcelona, with La Liga and EPL fixtures.
  2. Anime: done. One Piece, Jujutsu Kaisen, Demon Slayer.
  3. Your trails. Your home area or two or three favourite hikes.
  4. Your TV model, so I can check it's Android TV / Google TV before we touch anything.
  5. Mood. Keep the three time-of-day wallpapers, or stay on one.
