What makes a look

  • Scene: a full painted backdrop drawn in code, such as the stadium, the moon or the wave.
  • Hero: a player or character poster on top of the scene. It can be off, one fixed hero, or a new one every 5 minutes.
  • Watcher: something that follows the selected app. Eyes (Sharingan, Mangekyō, Six Eyes, Super Saiyan, Ultra Instinct, Nine-Tails, Sage Mode) blink as they look. Kakashi's Chidori zips to it, Itachi's crows fly to it, Law's ROOM wraps it, Robin's hands point at it, Sanji's hearts float to it, Chopper's hat and Rohit's bat turn to it. The seven Dragon Balls gather over it, the Power Pole extends to it, Nami's thundercloud drifts over it, Shinobu's butterflies fly to it, Luffy's hat turns to it and Zoro's swords point at it.
  • Wake, focus effect and clock, each chosen separately.
  • Moment: the big event the look reacts to, such as a goal, a six or a new episode. Real scores and episode releases set it off.

Mix any scene with any hero and eyes, then save the result as your own look.

On a real TV

Each scene is drawn once when it loads, and the moving parts on top (flashes, flags, crows, rain) are small and few. That keeps it smooth on a 1 GB TV. The Calm and Off motion settings reduce the movement further.

Player photos and official art can replace the drawn heroes on your own TV. The public repo ships only the code-drawn versions.

The den-tv repo

Each look is one small file in looks/, and the scenes, heroes, eyes and effects live in a shared library. So a new look is usually just a new mix, and friends can share theirs as a file or a QR code.
