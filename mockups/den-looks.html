Make your own character, no drawing needed

You make a few pictures with an AI image tool, and den-tv does the animating: the eyes follow the selected app, it blinks, the head tilts, and it breathes and walks along the dock. Everything happens in Customise → Your characters.

  1. Make the character. In Gemini, ChatGPT or another image tool, use prompt 1 from the prompt builder. Ask for a front view, eyes looking straight ahead, a plain white background and the whole body in frame. Pick the one you like best.
  2. Make the matching pictures in the same chat. Use prompt 2 for eyes closed (blinking) and prompt 3 for plain eyes with no pupils. Check that the pose and size really did stay the same; ask again if they moved.
  3. Remove the white background from each picture. Free options: Photopea (photopea.com, Image → Remove background), Canva's background remover, or your phone's photo app ("lift subject"). Save each as a PNG.
  4. Make the pupils picture. In Photopea, open the original, erase everything except the two pupils, and save it as pupils.png at the same size. The no-pupils picture from step 2 becomes your main character picture.
  5. Optional: a head that tilts. Erase everything except the head and save it as head.png, then erase the head from the main picture.
  6. Upload them in Customise → Your characters: character, pupils, eyes closed and head. Tap the middle of each eye, choose how far the eyes move, then press Save. Use it as the big figure or as a walker.

Tips: all pictures must be the same size and line up exactly. Under 1 MB each keeps a 1 GB TV fast. Only one picture? Upload just the character and it will still breathe, lean toward the selected app and walk. Most image tools won't draw named anime characters or real players, so describe the look instead, as the prompts do. Pictures of real people need their permission. Your characters stay on your TV and phone and never go into the den-tv repo.

Character prompt template
Full-body character illustration of [WHO, OUTFIT, EXPRESSION].
[STYLE]. Standing, front view, facing the viewer, eyes wide open
and looking straight ahead, arms slightly away from the body.
The whole character in frame from head to feet with some space
around. Even, flat lighting. Plain pure white background, no
ground shadow, no text. Portrait 9:16, high resolution.

What makes a look

  • Scene: a full painted backdrop drawn in code, such as the stadium, the moon or the wave.
  • Hero: a player or character poster on top of the scene. It can be off, one fixed hero, or a new one every 5 minutes.
  • Watcher: something that follows the selected app. Eyes (Sharingan, Mangekyō, Six Eyes, Super Saiyan, Ultra Instinct, Nine-Tails, Sage Mode) blink as they look. Kakashi's Chidori zips to it, Itachi's crows fly to it, Law's ROOM wraps it, Robin's hands point at it, Sanji's hearts float to it, Chopper's hat and Rohit's bat turn to it. The seven Dragon Balls gather over it, the Power Pole extends to it, Nami's thundercloud drifts over it, Shinobu's butterflies fly to it, Luffy's hat turns to it and Zoro's swords point at it.
  • Wake, focus effect and clock, each chosen separately.
  • App icons: a style (Dragon Balls, Wanted posters, manga ink, neon, jersey patches, enamel pins, scorecards, pixels, glass, stickers, HUD) and a layout (row, football formation, cricket field, Grand Line arc, bookshelf, big carousel, side rail). Live badges, smart order, a Shows folder and your own names and pictures are in Customise → App icons.
  • Moment: the big event the look reacts to, such as a goal, a six or a new episode. Real scores and episode releases set it off.

Mix any scene with any hero and eyes, then save the result as your own look.

On a real TV

Each scene is drawn once when it loads, and the moving parts on top (flashes, flags, crows, rain) are small and few. That keeps it smooth on a 1 GB TV. The Calm and Off motion settings reduce the movement further.

Player photos and official art can replace the drawn heroes on your own TV. The public repo ships only the code-drawn versions.

The den-tv repo

Each look is one small file in looks/, and the scenes, heroes, eyes and effects live in a shared library. So a new look is usually just a new mix, and friends can share theirs as a file or a QR code.
