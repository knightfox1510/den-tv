Can Messi, Virat and Rohit be on it?

Yes, on your own TV. You can put any picture you like on a TV in your home. Where it gets complicated is sharing: almost every good photo of a player belongs to a photographer or an agency, so it can't go into the public repo or a theme you hand around.

  • Official wallpapers: FC Barcelona, Mumbai Indians and the BCCI publish phone and desktop wallpapers for fans to use personally. I'd crop them to 16:9 and darken them.
  • Free-licence photos: Wikimedia Commons has CC-licensed photos of all three players. These can be shared, with a credit line.
  • Posters drawn in code, like the ones in this mockup: jersey numbers, club colours, names. They're yours to share anywhere.

The anime side works the same way. Crunchyroll and the studios post official key art you can use at home, and fan art needs the artist's permission.
